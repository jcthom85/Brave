# Brave Act 2 Production Batch: Chapter 1 - The Iron Road Blockade

## Intent
To transition the player from the Act 1 frontier into the Act 2 provincial heartland by introducing the physical and political blockade of the Iron Hegemony.

---

## 1. Rooms & World Topology
We need to extend the world beyond the `East Gate`.

- **`iron_road_ascent`**: A steep, winding stone path that climbs from the frontier mud up to the massive, elevated Iron Road.
- **`iron_road_toll_house`**: A cold, stone structure built into the road itself. Heavily guarded.
- **`garrison_outpost_4`**: A military camp adjacent to the Toll-House. Steam-pipes and iron fences.
- **`low_road_trailhead`**: A hidden, overgrown path that drops off the side of the Iron Road into the valleys below.

## 2. Quests
- **`the_iron_road_blockade` (Bridge Quest)**
    - **Giver:** Mayor Elric (Brambleford).
    - **Objective:** Reach the Iron Road Toll-House and present the "Frontier Pass."
    - **Summary:** The Hegemony has closed the border. Find out why and get past it.
- **`toll_of_the_censorate`**
    - **Giver:** Toll-Master Varick.
    - **Objective:** Pay 1,000 silver (impossible) or "acquire" a Hegemony Transit Permit from the Garrison.
    - **Summary:** The Toll-Master is enforcing a "Spiritual Quarantine." Without a permit, you aren't crossing.
- **`the_low_road_option`**
    - **Giver:** Kaelen the Smuggler (found in the shadows of the Toll-House).
    - **Objective:** Locate the Low-Road Trailhead and clear the "Road-Stalkers" blocking it.
    - **Summary:** If you can't go over, go under.

## 3. NPCs
- **`toll_master_varick`**:
    - **Location:** `iron_road_toll_house`
    - **Vibe:** Smug, bureaucratic, and dismissive of "frontier trash."
- **`corporal_jax`**:
    - **Location:** `garrison_outpost_4`
    - **Vibe:** Tired, cynical. He knows the quarantine is a lie but just wants to finish his shift.
- **`kaelen_the_smuggler`**:
    - **Location:** `iron_road_toll_house` (hiding)
    - **Vibe:** Shifty but competent. A secret sympathizer for the Vow-Keepers.

## 4. Enemies & Encounters
- **`hegemony_sentry`**: Standard armored guard.
- **`road_stalker`**: A lean, predatory beast that hunts the gaps in the Iron Road.
- **`siphon_drone`**: A small, floating mechanical eye that patrols for unauthorized travelers.

## 5. Items
- **`hegemony_transit_permit`**: (Story Item) A stamped iron plate allowing passage.
- **`smuggler_s_mark`**: (Loot) A small token that identifies the player to Vow-Keepers in Oakhaven.
- **`rusted_siphon_valve`**: (Material) A common drop from drones.
