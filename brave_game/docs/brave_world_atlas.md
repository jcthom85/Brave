# Brave World Atlas: The Known Domains

## The Scale of the World
The world of Brave is designed as a **massive continental landmass** connected by the "Iron Road." In a text-based MMORPG, the "size" is less about square mileage and more about **density of interaction**. The world is divided into four primary tiers (corresponding to our Acts), each roughly the size of a small country.

---

## 1. The Frontier (Act 1 Zone)
**Primary Hub:** **Brambleford**
*   **Scale:** A cluster of small villages, outposts, and wilderness.
*   **Other Settlements:**
    *   **Wayfarer’s Yard:** The tutorial training camp.
    *   **South-Reach:** A tiny, mostly abandoned fishing hamlet on the edge of the Blackfen.
    *   **The Ridge Camps:** Temporary camps for road crews and loggers.
*   **Vibe:** Isolated, practical, and vulnerable. The nearest major town is weeks away on foot, making Brambleford’s independence both a pride and a peril.

## 2. The Provincial Heartland (Act 2 Zone)
**Primary Hub:** **Oakhaven** (The City of Siphons)
*   **Scale:** A sprawling urban center surrounded by agricultural "Bread-Baskets" and mining towns.
*   **Other Settlements:**
    *   **Iron-Garrison 4:** A military town built specifically to guard the Toll-House and the border.
    *   **The Rust-Hills:** A collection of vertical mining "stacks" where families live and die without ever seeing a green field.
    *   **Greystone:** A decaying trade town that used to be a hub before the Iron Road bypass made it obsolete.
*   **Vibe:** Industrial, crowded, and politically charged. This is where players first see the "civilized" face of the Hegemony.

## 3. The Scarred Lands (Act 3 Zone)
**Primary Hub:** **The Last Outpost** (The "Edge of the World")
*   **Scale:** A vast, hostile wilderness of ash, glass, and ancient ruins.
*   **Other Settlements:**
    *   **Vow-Seeker Encampments:** Small, nomadic groups of rebels and scholars hiding in the ruins.
    *   **Extraction Rig 01:** A massive, moving industrial town operated by the Hegemony to mine the First Cage.
    *   **The Echoing Monolith:** A "ghost town" inhabited only by the archived spirits of the ancient kings.
*   **Vibe:** Alien, dangerous, and atmospheric. Survival mechanics (heat, ash-storms) are paramount here.

## 4. The Imperial Core (Act 4 Zone)
**Primary Hub:** **Aethelgard** (The Eternal City)
*   **Scale:** A hyper-dense "Mega-City" built around the Spire of Ascension.
*   **Other Settlements:**
    *   **The Floating Gardens:** Luxury villas for the High Architects.
    *   **The Bastion of Truth:** The central fortress of the Iron Legion.
    *   **The Undercity:** A subterranean world of siphoned slaves and technical waste.
*   **Vibe:** Opulent, blindingly bright, and oppressive. This is the heart of the "Big Lie."

---

## Travel Infrastructure: The Iron Road
The world is stitched together by the **Iron Road**—a massive, elevated stone highway built using ancient Vow-technology.
*   **Fast Travel:** Players eventually unlock "Lumen-Carts" or "Winged Relay" systems (limited fast travel) once they restore the local wards in a region.
*   **The Blockade:** In Act 2, the Iron Road is physically closed by the Hegemony, forcing players to find "Low-Roads" (dangerous wilderness paths) or sewer networks to move between major regions.

## Horizontal Expansion (The MMORPG "Density")
Because this is an MMORPG, each "Act" is not a linear path but a **Zone**.
*   **Side Towns:** Each zone has 2-3 smaller "Minor Hubs" with their own shops, local quest lines, and unique resources (e.g., unique fish in the Rust-Hills, unique crafting ores in the Scarred Lands).
*   **Dungeons/Raids:** Scattered between these towns are "Points of Interest" (POIs) that serve as instanced or public dungeon content.
