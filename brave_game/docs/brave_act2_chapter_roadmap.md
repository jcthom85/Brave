# Brave Act 2: The Hollowed Heart - Chapter Roadmap

## Act Overview
Act 2 takes the player from the isolated frontier of Brambleford into the decaying provincial heartland. Here, the struggle is no longer just against wild beasts and goblins, but against the bureaucratic indifference and active malice of the Iron Hegemony’s regional administration.

---

## Chapter 1: The Iron Road Blockade
**Focus:** Transition and Travel.
**Goal:** Reach the provincial border.
**Story:** Brambleford is safe (for now), but supply lines are still cut. The player must travel the "Iron Road"—a massive, elevated stone highway—to reach the capital. However, a Hegemony "Tax Garrison" has closed the road, claiming "spiritual quarantine."
**Key Locations:** Iron Road Toll-House, The Rust-Hills, Garrison Outpost 4.
**Mystery:** Why is the Hegemony stopping supplies from *leaving* the fertile capital?

## Chapter 2: The Gates of Oakhaven
**Focus:** Urban Exploration and Social Disparity.
**Goal:** Gain entry to the Provincial Capital.
**Story:** Oakhaven is a city of two halves: the "Gilded Spire" where the lights never flicker, and the "Low-Quarter" which is swamped in unnatural fog and darkness. The player must navigate the slums and find a way past the city’s magical censors.
**Key Locations:** The Silt Slums, Gate of the Grand Architect, The Fog-Docks.
**Mystery:** The "Fog" in the Low-Quarter feels alive—and it smells like the Blackfen.

## Chapter 3: The Archive of Truth
**Focus:** Stealth and Investigation.
**Goal:** Find the missing maintenance records for Brambleford’s South Light.
**Story:** To prove the lights are being sabotaged, the player must infiltrate the Hegemony’s central library. They discover that all records of the "Primal Sovereigns" have been scrubbed or altered.
**Key Locations:** The Hall of Records, The Redacted Wing, The Curator’s Secret Study.
**Mystery:** Why do the Drowned Weir maintenance orders and Lower Lanternworks work records name Foreman Coilback as if the south-line duty was still being enforced? (Evidence found in Act 1).

## Chapter 4: The Siphon’s Shadow
**Focus:** Dungeon Crawl and Technical Revelation.
**Goal:** Locate the source of the capital’s power.
**Story:** Beneath the Gilded Spire lies a massive industrial complex. The player finds "Siphon-Conduits"—black pipes that pulse with stolen primal energy, literally draining the life from the surrounding provinces to power the city’s elite.
**Key Locations:** The Under-Spire, Siphon Intake-A, The Cooling Vats.
**Mystery:** The pipes aren't just carrying energy; they are carrying *screams*.

## Chapter 5: The Vow-Keepers
**Focus:** Faction Recruitment and Resistance.
**Goal:** Connect with the underground rebellion.
**Story:** The player is hunted by the High Censor’s "Silencers." They are rescued by the Vow-Keepers, a group of former lamplighters and scholars who still remember the old rituals.
**Key Locations:** The Hidden Lamp-House (Resistance HQ), Old Sewer Tunnels.
**Mystery:** The leader of the Vow-Keepers is someone from the player’s past (or a known Act 1 NPC’s relative).

## Chapter 6: The Siege of the Censorate
**Focus:** Act Capstone and Major Conflict.
**Goal:** Defeat High Censor Valerius and expose the Siphon.
**Story:** A coordinated strike on the Censorate tower. The player must navigate the High Censor’s "Truth-Fields" (magical illusions) to reach him.
**Key Locations:** The Spire of Certainty, The High Censor’s Balcony.
**Mystery:** Valerius’s final words reveal that he is just a "middle-manager" for the Arch-Chancellor in the Imperial Capital.

---

## Act 2 Success Criteria
*   The player realizes the "Crisis" is an intentional policy of the Hegemony.
*   The player learns the first "Reclamation Ritual" to temporarily heal a Siphoned area.
*   The world map expands to include the Provincial Heartland.
