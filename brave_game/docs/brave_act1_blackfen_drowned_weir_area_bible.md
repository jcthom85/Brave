## Story Job

Blackfen teaches that the crisis has seeped into land, water, and light.

Drowned Weir teaches that the wrong south light is not folklore. It is a broken public work still enforcing a duty no living person understands.

The player should learn:

- Blackfen is wet, physical, hungry, and hard to map.
- boglights mislead before the lanternworks explain why.
- Miretooth is the apex predator warning sign, not the final answer.
- Drowned Weir is civic infrastructure first and mythic evidence second.
- the Hollow Lantern ends Brambleford's first hard chapter without revealing the full Broken Vow.

## Content Rules

Good Blackfen additions:

- survey markers, field-note recovery, reed maps, marsh-guide hooks, carrion clues.
- modest items: marsh survey tags, silt hooks, fen resin, jaw-score tracings, reed maps.
- encounter variants using blackfen, hound, mire, crow, plant, spirit, or beast roles.

Good Drowned Weir additions:

- maintenance maps, lensglass, ward clamps, signal scraps, road-light ledgers.
- readable technical notes that explain old machinery through use and danger.
- modest items: lens shards, stamped clamps, blackwater keymarks, lamp coils, signal scraps.
- encounter variants using existing weir constructs, warders, coils, and blackwater threats.

Avoid:

- making Blackfen purely spooky or spectral.
- adding another apex predator that competes with Miretooth.
- revealing the full Iron Hegemony or Primal Sovereign truth in Act 1.
- making the Hollow Lantern a talking villain.
- adding a bigger capstone beyond the Hollow Lantern.
