## Story Job

Whispering Woods teaches that the land around Brambleford is arranged, not merely wild.

The player should learn:

- Brambleford depends on the woods for medicine and routes.
- the old stones were placed for a civic or protective purpose.
- the woods are not a fae kingdom; they are a local system becoming unreliable.
- the barrow field is connected by warning signs, not exposition.

The emotional shape should be:

1. The woods seem like ordinary danger after Ruk.
2. Herbs and tracks show the town still depends on this place.
3. Wardstones and silence imply an older boundary is failing.
4. Greymaw or the old path points the player toward barrow trouble.

## Content Rules

Good additions:

- wardstone investigation, trail marker recovery, herb verification, and Maybelle/Mira follow-ups.
- readable warnings on stones, bark strips, chapel chalk, or old path markers.
- modest items: ward chalk, moonleaf bundles, briar twine, path tokens, poultice ingredients.
- encounter variants using beasts, spiders, mosslings, briar imps, wolves, or old protective remnants.
- dialogue that keeps the fear grounded in medicine, paths, and family safety.

Avoid:

- forest courts, royal fae politics, or a separate woodland campaign.
- another major woods boss competing with Greymaw.
- solving the mystery of the Vow here.
- making the woods purely haunted; they must stay physical, damp, thorny, and useful.
