# Brave Act 2 Agent Bible: The Hollowed Heart

## Purpose
This document provides the creative guidance for generating Act 2 content. While Act 1 was about local survival in Brambleford, Act 2 is about **Regional Conspiracy**, **Industrial Decay**, and the **Malice of the Hegemony**.

## Act 2 Vision
The player leaves the green (if troubled) frontier and enters a world of verticality, iron, and smoke. The "Hollowed Heart" refers to the provincial capital Oakhaven, which is beautiful on the surface but literally hollowed out underneath by siphoning machinery.

The emotional arc:
- "The town's problems were just a symptom of a larger rot."
- "The people in charge are not just indifferent; they are the cause."
- "There is a hidden movement (Vow-Keepers) that knows the truth."

## Core Rules for Act 2
- **Industrial Texture:** Descriptions should include iron, steam, blackwater, gears, siphons, and "Truth-Fields" (Hegemony illusions).
- **Social Disparity:** Contrast the opulence of Hegemony officials with the "Grey Sickness" of the workers in the Rust-Hills.
- **The Censors:** Hegemony enemies are organized, bureaucratic, and speak in "Corporate/Civic" jargon. They don't just kill; they "redact" or "quarantine."
- **Siphoning Mechanics:** Content should reflect that the land is "Thin" here. Magic (rituals) is harder to perform unless the player finds a "Primal Leak."

## Major Factions
1.  **The Office of Civic Truth (The Censors):** The secret police. They wear white iron masks and carry "Redaction Staves."
2.  **The Vow-Keepers:** An underground network of scholars and former lamplighters. They use "Hollow-Signs" (secret symbols) to mark safe houses.
3.  **The Iron Road Crews:** Hardened workers who maintain the highways. They are caught in the middle—resentful of the Hegemony but terrified of the Fraying.

---

## Region 1: The Iron Road (Transition Zone)
**Role:** To show the scale of the world and the reality of the blockade.
**Key Features:** Elevated stone highways, massive toll-gates, and "Garrison Outposts."
**Allowed Content:**
- Quests about smuggling, bribing guards, or finding "Low-Road" animal trails.
- Enemies like "Road-Stalkers" (predators that hunt the highways) and "Garrison Sentries."

## Region 2: The Rust-Hills (Industrial Slums)
**Role:** To show the human cost of the siphoning.
**Key Features:** Vertical mining stacks, smoke-filled canyons, and "Rust-Lung" clinics.
**Allowed Content:**
- Quests about industrial sabotage, recovering "stolen" (reclaimed) ore, and protecting sick workers.
- Enemies like "Siphon-Leeches" (parasites drawn to the machinery) and "Overseer Drones."

## Region 3: Oakhaven (The Hub)
**Role:** The massive urban hub of Act 2.
**Key Features:** The Gilded Spire (High-Quarter) and the Silt Slums (Low-Quarter).
**Allowed Content:**
- Quests involving urban stealth, library infiltration (Hall of Records), and meeting Vow-Keeper contacts in the sewers.
- Items like "Censor Seals," "Siphon-Keys," and "Redacted Ledgers."

## Enemy Tag Families
- **Hegemony:** `censor`, `sentry`, `drone`, `archivist`, `silencer`
- **Industrial:** `construct`, `leech`, `worker`, `overseer`
- **Resistance:** `vowkeeper`, `rebel`, `scout`

## Item Rarity & Tone
Act 2 gear moves from "Hand-made/Repaired" to "Siphoned/Industrial."
- **Common:** Scrap Iron, Siphon-Grease, Worker’s Tally.
- **Uncommon:** Censor Signet, Reinforced Plating, Lens-Focus.
- **Story:** "The First Hollow-Sign," "The Redacted Ledger of Brambleford," "Valerius’s Siphon-Key."
