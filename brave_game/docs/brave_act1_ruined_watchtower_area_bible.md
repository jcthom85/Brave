## Purpose

The Ruined Watchtower is Act 1's mortal threat region. This document keeps future Creator API content focused on road control, opportunism, tactical pressure, and the cost of ordinary people exploiting a crisis.

## Story Job

The Ruined Watchtower teaches that not every threat is ancient or magical.

The player should learn:

- Brambleford's old border structures can be turned against it.
- bandits understand sightlines, dogs, pressure, and supply control.
- Blackreed is dangerous because he is competent, not because he is mythic.
- mortal greed and command failure can worsen supernatural panic.

## Content Rules

Good additions:

- prisoner, scout, signal, stolen-ledger, and supply-cache quests.
- readable ledgers, road tallies, signal marks, arrow tags, old watchtower records.
- modest items: blackreed ledger, snapped signal arrow, watch token, road tally, stolen cargo label.
- encounter variants using bandit, blackreed, archer, scout, commander, hound, or trap-adjacent roles.
- dialogue that treats Blackreed as a local commander exploiting chaos.

Avoid:

- making Blackreed part of a national army.
- turning the tower into a second town hub.
- adding another tower boss.
- over-explaining the Iron Hegemony through bandit notes.

## Creator API Batch Brief

- one quest

- Title: "The Ridge Tally"
- Giver: Captain Harl Rowan

- The tower reads as a repurposed border structure.
- Blackreed remains the capstone.
- The content clarifies road control without expanding the main conspiracy too early.
