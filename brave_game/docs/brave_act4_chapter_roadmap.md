# Brave Act 4: The Seat of the Lie - Chapter Roadmap

## Act Overview
Act 4 is the climax of the Truth-Binder’s journey. The mystery has been solved, the lie exposed, and the consequences understood. Now, the player must lead a rebellion against the heart of the Iron Hegemony: the Eternal City of Aethelgard. This act is focused on high-stakes conflict, large-scale cooperation, and the ultimate moral choice of the game.

---

## Chapter 1: The Gathering of the Unbound
**Focus:** Faction Management and World State Change.
**Goal:** Assemble the regional factions for the march on the capital.
**Story:** The player must travel back through Brambleford, Oakhaven, and the Scarred Lands to rally the people. The world state changes: Hegemony forces are now openly hostile everywhere.
**Key Locations:** The War-Council at Oakhaven, The Vanguard Camp.
**Mystery:** Some "allies" might be Hegemony spies. Who can you truly trust?

## Chapter 2: The Eternal City, Aethelgard
**Focus:** High-Level Combat and Aesthetic Contrast.
**Goal:** Breach the outer walls of the capital.
**Story:** Aethelgard is breathtakingly beautiful, lit by "Celestial Lanterns" that burn so bright they blind the poor. The player sees the absolute peak of what the stolen energy can buy: a paradise built on a dying world.
**Key Locations:** The Golden Gates, The Avenue of Ascension, The Hanging Gardens of Light.
**Mystery:** There are no children in Aethelgard. Why?

## Chapter 3: The Iron Legion’s Last Stand
**Focus:** Military Campaign and Elite Bosses.
**Goal:** Defeat the Censor-General and the Imperial Guard.
**Story:** The player leads the charge into the military heart of the city. They face the "Iron Legion"—soldiers who have been "Augmented" with siphoned energy, becoming more machine than man.
**Key Locations:** The Bastion of Truth, The Censor-General’s War-Room.
**Mystery:** The Censor-General is actually a "Vow-Relic" brought to life—a puppet of the Arch-Chancellor.

## Chapter 4: The Spire of Ascension
**Focus:** The Ultimate Dungeon.
**Goal:** Climb the Spire to reach the Arch-Chancellor.
**Story:** The Spire is a vertical factory of cosmic power. Each floor represents a different layer of the "Siphon." The air is thick with the raw essence of the Primal Sovereigns.
**Key Locations:** The Siphon-Core, The Hall of False Gods, The Gravity-Well.
**Mystery:** The Spire isn't just a power plant; it’s a *ship*.

## Chapter 5: The Arch-Chancellor’s Truth
**Focus:** Final Boss and Philosophical Confrontation.
**Goal:** Defeat the Arch-Chancellor.
**Story:** The Arch-Chancellor reveals the final truth: the world *cannot* be saved. The Vow was always a death-sentence, and "Ascension" is the only way for *any* part of humanity to survive. They aren't a villain in their own eyes, but a tragic savior.
**Key Locations:** The Observatory of the Void, The Chancellor’s Seat.
**Mystery:** The Arch-Chancellor is the one who *signed* the original Vow a thousand years ago.

## Chapter 6: The Choice of the Vow
**Focus:** Endgame Resolution and World-Altering Event.
**Goal:** Decide the fate of the world.
**Story:** With the Arch-Chancellor defeated, the player stands at the controls of the Spire.
*   **Choice A (Restore the Vow):** Sacrifice the Spire’s power to heal the land. The Hegemony falls, technology reverts, but the world survives.
*   **Choice B (Complete Ascension):** Take the Spire and the elite into the void, leaving the world to the Primal Sovereigns (A "New Game Plus" or "Reboot" hook).
*   **Choice C (The Third Path):** Break the Spire, free the Sovereigns, and let the chaos decide.
**Key Locations:** The Heart of the World.

---

## Act 4 Success Criteria
*   The Iron Hegemony is dismantled.
*   The Player achieves the rank of "Grand Truth-Binder."
*   The main story arc of the "Broken Vow" reaches a definitive conclusion.
