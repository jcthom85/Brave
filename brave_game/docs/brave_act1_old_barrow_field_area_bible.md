# Brave Act 1 Area Bible: Old Barrow Field

## Purpose

Old Barrow Field is Act 1's first heavier emotional region. This document defines how to add chapel, memory, and grave-marker content without turning the region into generic necromancy.

## Story Job

Old Barrow Field teaches that Brambleford's protections include memory, names, prayer, and burial respect.

The player should learn:

- the dead are failed rest and broken duty, not random monsters.
- the chapel is useful but does not fully understand the old systems.
- names matter mechanically and emotionally.
- Sir Edric is the region's answer; do not create another fallen-knight equivalent.

The emotional shape should be:

1. The lanterns fail after dusk.
2. The player sees names and old duties losing their hold.
3. The chapel asks for proof, not blind certainty.
4. Sir Edric gives the region a face and a conclusion.

## Content Rules

Good additions:

- marker readings, chapel records, recovered name strips, votives, grave-light clues.
- side quests that confirm a name, return proof, relight a marker, or carry a rite.
- modest items: grave tokens, cracked votives, chapel wax, name strips, old bell tags.
- encounter variants using undead, wisp, shade, skeleton, knight, or support tags.
- dialogue that admits uncertainty without becoming helpless.

Avoid:

- a necromancer explanation unless a later Act 1 pass gives local evidence.
- another tragic knight boss.
- jokes that undercut burial respect.
- turning the chapel into a magical answer machine.

## Creator API Batch Brief

Next recommended run: **Old Barrow Chapel and Marker Names**.

Create one small side quest tying one erased marker to Brother Alden's chapel records. The quest should make the undead feel like people whose rest has been disturbed, not just an enemy type.

Required shape:

- one quest
- one readable marker or chapel record
- one proof item
- Brother Alden active and completed dialogue
- no new boss
- no new region

Preferred quest concept:

- Title: "Names Under Dusk"
- Giver: Brother Alden
- Action: inspect Marker Row, recover a loosened name strip or votive, and return it to the chapel.
- Payoff: Alden can name one of the restless dead before asking the player to keep pushing toward Sir Edric.

Acceptance:

- The barrows feel personal before the capstone.
- The chapel remains brave, limited, and human.
- The quest strengthens `the_knight_without_rest` instead of competing with it.
