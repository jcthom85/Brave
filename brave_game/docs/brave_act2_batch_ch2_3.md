# Brave Act 2 Production Batch: Chapters 2 & 3 - Oakhaven Entry

## Intent
To introduce the player to the urban reality of the Hegemony, focusing on the social disparity of Oakhaven and the hidden history found in its archives.

---

## 1. Rooms & World Topology
### Chapter 2: The Gates of Oakhaven
- **`oakhaven_outer_walls`**: Massive white-stone walls pulsing with a faint blue light (The Truth-Field). 
- **`the_silt_slums`**: The "Low-Quarter." Muddy streets, makeshift wooden shacks built into the sewer grates, and the ever-present "Blackfen Fog."
- **`the_fog_docks`**: Where blackwater barges bring in siphoned materials from the frontier.
- **`gate_of_the_grand_architect`**: The main entrance to the High-Quarter. Blocked by a "Lumen-Censor."

### Chapter 3: The Archive of Truth
- **`hall_of_records_foyer`**: Grand, echoey, and filled with "Quiet-Drones."
- **`the_redacted_wing`**: A section of the library where the shelves are literally empty, but the shadows move.
- **`curator_secret_study`**: A hidden room behind a rotating shelf in the Hall of Records.

---

## 2. Quests
### Chapter 2 Quests
- **`eyes_in_the_fog`**
    - **Giver:** Mother Grendel (Silt Slums).
    - **Objective:** Recover 5 "Drowned Scraps" from the Fog-Docks to build a "Fog-Lantern."
    - **Summary:** The mist in the slums isn't natural. You need protection to find your way.
- **`the_architect_s_price`**
    - **Giver:** Gate-Warden Thorne (No relation to Elric).
    - **Objective:** Deliver a "Letter of Recommendation" from a High-Quarter citizen (obtained by bribing or helping someone in the slums).
    - **Summary:** Money isn't enough to enter the High-Quarter; you need status.

### Chapter 3 Quests
- **`the_missing_ledger`**
    - **Giver:** Kaelen (reappearing).
    - **Objective:** Infiltrate the Hall of Records and find the "Brambleford Maintenance Tally."
    - **Summary:** Proof of the sabotage is hidden in plain sight.
- **`shadow_of_the_censor`**
    - **Giver:** Anonymous Note.
    - **Objective:** Avoid the "Quiet-Drones" in the Redacted Wing and reach the Curator’s Study.
    - **Summary:** Stealth mission. If the drones see you, the "Truth-Field" will expel you.

---

## 3. NPCs
- **`mother_grendel`**:
    - **Location:** `the_silt_slums`
    - **Vibe:** Blind but "sees" through the fog. A local healer who remembers the old ways.
- **`gate_warden_thorne`**:
    - **Location:** `gate_of_the_grand_architect`
    - **Vibe:** Corrupt, bored, and obsessed with "Civic Purity."
- **`curator_elara`**:
    - **Location:** `curator_secret_study`
    - **Vibe:** Nervous, brilliant. She has been secretly saving redacted books.

---

## 4. Enemies & Encounters
- **`fog_lurker`**: A distorted, semi-transparent beast that lives in the Low-Quarter mist.
- **`quiet_drone`**: A floating mechanical bell. Non-lethal but triggers "Truth-Field" teleportation (stuns/resets the room).
- **`censor_archivist`**: A human scholar armed with "Ink-Blot" spells that blind the player.

---

## 5. Items
- **`fog_lantern`**: (Tool) Allows the player to see exits in `the_silt_slums` and `the_fog_docks`.
- **`redacted_page`**: (Loot) A scrap of paper that reveals one small piece of "True History."
- **`curator_s_spectacles`**: (Accessory) Increases 'Investigation' or 'Detection' stats.
