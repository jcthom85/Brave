# Brave Expansions Roadmap: The Path of the Truth-Binder

## Overview
This roadmap outlines the first four major Acts (Expansions) of Brave. Each expansion represents a significant jump in power, scale, and narrative revelation, providing a "Russian Doll" of mysteries that players peel back as they progress.

---

## Act 1: The Fraying Edge (The Frontier)
**Location:** Brambleford and its immediate surroundings (Whispering Woods, Blackfen, Drowned Weir).
**Theme:** Grounded Local Crisis.
**The Player's Role:** A useful hand helping a struggling town.
**The Reveal:** The South Light (Hollow Lantern) wasn't just "broken"; it was being fed a specific frequency of energy to drain the local ward network.
**Major Boss:** The Hollow Lantern (A corrupted civic machine).
**Meta-Progress:** Player earns the trust of the local Watch and Chapel.

## Act 2: The Hollowed Heart (The Provinces)
**Location:** Oakhaven (The Provincial Capital) and the Iron-Road hubs.
**Theme:** Urban Decay and Political Conspiracy.
**The Player's Role:** A regional operative investigating why the "Lumen-Crews" aren't coming to help the frontier.
**The Reveal:** The Provincial Governor is a "Siphon-Lord" who is deliberately starving the frontier towns to keep the capital’s lights burning. The "Grand Architects" aren't gods, just high-ranking bureaucrats with ancient tech.
**Major Boss:** High Censor Valerius (Leader of the secret police in Oakhaven).
**Meta-Progress:** Player joins the underground "Vow-Keeper" resistance.

## Act 3: The Scarred Lands (The Ancient Zones)
**Location:** The Silent Waste and the Barrows of the First Kings.
**Theme:** Environmental Storytelling and Mythic Ruins.
**The Player's Role:** A seeker of ancient truth venturing into "Red Zones" where the Hegemony forbids travel.
**The Reveal:** Finding the first "Cage." The Primal Sovereigns are still alive, imprisoned in titanic machinery that powers the Hegemony's immortality. The "Broken Vow" was a betrayal of a literal person/spirit.
**Major Boss:** The Jailer of the First Cage (A mechanical-undead monstrosity).
**Meta-Progress:** Player learns the "Language of the Vow" (New skill system).

## Act 4: The Seat of the Lie (The Imperial Capital)
**Location:** Aethelgard (The Eternal City) and the Spire of Ascension.
**Theme:** High Fantasy Rebellion and End-of-the-World Stakes.
**The Player's Role:** A mythic Truth-Binder leading an army of the dispossessed and liberated spirits.
**The Reveal:** The Hegemony's "Ascension" is a plan to tear a hole in reality and flee, which will cause the "Great Collapse" and destroy everyone left behind.
**Major Boss:** The Arch-Chancellor (The one who originally broke the Vow).
**Meta-Progress:** The choice to restore the Vow (Sacrifice) or forge a new path (Unknown).

---

## Act 5 and Beyond: The New Vow
Future content can explore:
*   **The Wild Reclaim:** Dealing with a world where the Primal Sovereigns are free but angry.
*   **The Other Continents:** How did other cultures handle the Breaking?
*   **The Void:** Exploring where the Hegemony tried to flee.
