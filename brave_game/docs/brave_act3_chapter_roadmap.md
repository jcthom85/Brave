# Brave Act 3: The Scarred Lands - Chapter Roadmap

## Act Overview
Act 3 shifts from the political corruption of the cities to the mythic consequences of the Broken Vow. Players venture into the "Silent Waste," a region where the Vow was first shattered. This is a land of extreme environmental hazards, ancient ghosts, and titanic ruins that defy the Hegemony's sanitized history.

---

## Chapter 1: Crossing the Dead-Zone
**Focus:** Survival and Navigation.
**Goal:** Survive the journey into the Silent Waste.
**Story:** The Hegemony has declared the Scarred Lands a "Divine Exclusion Zone." The player must find a way to cross the "Ashen Barrier"—a wall of literal gray death where the land has stopped breathing.
**Key Locations:** The Last Outpost, The Ash-Drifts, The Glass-Desert.
**Mystery:** The "Grey Death" isn't a disease; it's a lack of *memory* in the land.

## Chapter 2: The Echoes of the First Kings
**Focus:** Lore and Puzzle-Solving.
**Goal:** Awaken an ancient "Relay" to navigate the deep waste.
**Story:** The player explores the barrows of the kings who *signed* the Vow. They find that the kings didn't die—they were "archived" into the stone itself to serve as eternal witnesses.
**Key Locations:** The Tomb of the Three Crowns, The Whispering Obelisk.
**Mystery:** One of the kings is missing from their tomb. Where did they go?

## Chapter 3: The First Cage
**Focus:** Large-Scale Dungeon and Revelation.
**Goal:** Infiltrate the massive structure at the heart of the waste.
**Story:** The player finds a "Hegemony Extraction Rig" built on top of a subterranean temple. Inside is the **First Cage**, containing a fragment of the *Sovereign of Earth*. The creature is being milked for its essence like a battery.
**Key Locations:** Extraction Rig 01, The Chamber of the Bound Sovereign.
**Mystery:** The machine keeping the Sovereign bound was designed by someone who *loved* it.

## Chapter 4: The Language of the Vow
**Focus:** Skill Acquisition and Spirit Communication.
**Goal:** Learn to speak with the Bound Sovereign.
**Story:** The player cannot simply "kill" the machine; doing so would kill the Sovereign. They must learn the "Vow-Tongue" to stabilize the spirit's essence while they dismantle the Hegemony's siphon.
**Key Locations:** The Hall of Resonance, The Echoing Well.
**Mystery:** The Vow-Tongue sounds exactly like the player's own internal thoughts.

## Chapter 5: The Jailer’s Vigil
**Focus:** Act Capstone and Boss Fight.
**Goal:** Defeat the Jailer and liberate (partially) the Sovereign.
**Story:** The Jailer is a "High Architect" who has stayed alive for centuries by consuming the Sovereign's pain. Defeating them allows the player to "Rebind" a portion of the land, creating a permanent oasis of life in the waste.
**Key Locations:** The Jailer’s Spire, The Heart of the Machine.
**Mystery:** The Jailer thanks the player as they die. "Finally, a witness."

---

## Act 3 Success Criteria
*   The player confirms the "Gods" are literal, imprisoned beings.
*   The player unlocks the "Vow-Tongue" system for advanced interactions.
*   The world map expands to include the Scarred Lands/Silent Waste.
