# Brave Act 1 Area Bible: Goblin Road

## Purpose

Goblin Road is the first true adventure space after Brambleford. This document defines how to expand it through Creator API runs without diluting its job: make the opening alarm feel traceable, physical, and solved by the time Ruk falls.

Use this with `brave_act1_agent_bible.md`, `brave_act1_content_roadmap.md`, and `creator_agent_contract.md`.

## Story Job

Goblin Road teaches that Brambleford's first crisis is not random. The road is being dismantled on purpose.

The player should learn:

- fences are infrastructure, not scenery.
- wolves and goblins overlap because the town's protections are weakening.
- goblins are organized enough to scout, strip, steal, and report.
- Ruk is the first named culprit, not the Act 1 mastermind.

The emotional shape should be:

1. Brambleford hears trouble on the road.
2. The player sees physical damage and follows the pattern.
3. The player stops the crews and identifies Ruk as the local answer.
4. Ruk's defeat makes the road breathe again but leaves the black south-light question open.

## Content Rules

Good additions:

- road repair, patrol, scout, and proof quests.
- readable signs, wagon scraps, road ledgers, field marks, and fence tallies.
- modest items: repair tokens, stamped nails, rail marks, road charms, stolen store labels.
- encounter variants using existing goblin, wolf, cutter, raider, skirmisher, or brute roles.
- completed-state dialogue that lets Brambleford acknowledge the road is safer.

Avoid:

- a second Goblin Road boss.
- making Ruk a prophet, cult leader, or hidden servant of the Iron Hegemony.
- large lore dumps about the Broken Vow.
- new rooms unless a later pass explicitly expands the road.
- comic goblin content that makes the damage feel harmless.

## Creator API Batch Brief

Next recommended run: **Goblin Road Repair Thread**.

Create one small side quest after `fencebreakers` and before or after `ruk_the_fence_cutter`. The quest should ask the player to recover or document proof of road damage, then return that proof to Mira, Harl, or Mayor Elric.

Required shape:

- one quest
- one practical item used as proof or reward
- dialogue for the chosen giver in active and completed states
- no new rooms
- no new boss

Preferred quest concept:

- Title: "Rails for the Return"
- Giver: Mira Fenleaf or Mayor Elric Thorne
- Action: inspect the Old Fence Line and Fencebreaker Camp, recover marked fence nails or a road tally, and return proof so Brambleford can begin repairs.
- Payoff: the town understands the road was deliberately opened and darkened, not merely raided.

Acceptance:

- The road reads as a wounded public work.
- Ruk remains the capstone.
- The reward is modest and useful, not legendary.
