## Story Job

Goblin Warrens teaches that the road-cutters have a domestic system beneath the visible raids.

The player should learn:

- Ruk's camp was a gate, not the whole operation.
- hunger, stolen stores, and crude cooking shape goblin organization.
- goblin systems are functional and vivid, not noble or elaborate.
- Grubnak the Pot-King is the regional capstone.

## Content Rules

Good additions:

- stolen food, tunnel cleanup, crude recipe, prisoner scrap, and hex-token quests.
- readable grease notes, cooking marks, tally bones, stolen store labels.
- modest items: pot tokens, hex scraps, flour sacks, bone ladles, crude recipes, battered tools.
- encounter variants using goblin, warren, hexer, bat, slime, brute, or potking roles.
- dialogue that connects goblin hunger back to Brambleford's stores.

Avoid:

- a deeper goblin king beyond Grubnak in Act 1.
- making goblin culture polished, courtly, or noble.
- joke-only gross-out content.
- a second underground region before Act 1 is complete.

- New loot has placement or use.
