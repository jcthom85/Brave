# Brave Global Story Bible: The Broken Vow

## Purpose
This document serves as the absolute creative source of truth for the overarching narrative of Brave. It defines the meta-plot, the central mystery, and the "Big Bad" that links every Act together, ensuring that as the game scales, the content remains cohesive and the mysteries remain rewarding to solve.

## The Core Concept: The Broken Vow
The world of Brave does not run on "magic" in the traditional sense, but on **Pacts**. Long ago, the first civilizations realized they could not survive the raw, primordial chaos of the land. They struck a Vow with the *Primal Sovereigns* (the sentient, ancient spirit of the world).

*   **The Vow:** Humanity would provide stewardship, ritual blood/memory, and a portion of their labor to maintain the world’s balance. 
*   **The Reward:** The Sovereigns provided the "Lumen-Nodes" (Lanterns, Wards, and civic machinery) that held back the dark, stabilized the weather, and ensured the roads were safe.

## True History vs. Taught History

### The Taught History (The Public Lie)
*   **The Narrative:** The "Grand Architects" of the Central Hegemony built the Lanterns to save humanity from "The Primitive Night." The Hegemony is the sole source of order and light.
*   **The Crisis:** The current decay is a random "natural disaster" or caused by "barbaric goblin saboteurs" and "heretical cults." The Hegemony is working tirelessly to fix it, but needs more taxes, more labor, and absolute obedience.

### The True History (The Hidden Truth)
*   **The Narrative:** Centuries ago, the Hegemony’s High Council sought to "Optimize" the world. They grew tired of the rituals and the "tax" of stewardship required by the Primal Sovereigns. They found a way to "Siphon" the Lumen-Nodes directly, bypassing the Sovereigns and stealing the power for their own immortality and industrial expansion.
*   **The Breaking:** This act broke the Vow. The Primal Sovereigns withdrew their protection and turned their fury upon the world. The Hegemony managed to "Cage" the remaining Sovereigns in secret locations, but the cages are failing. The decay (The Fraying) is the land itself trying to reclaim its stolen power and wipe the human "parasites" away.

## The Big Bad: The Iron Hegemony
The primary antagonist is not a single monster, but a system.
*   **The Goal:** To finish siphoning every drop of primal power before the world collapses, then "Ascend" to a new plane, leaving the rest of humanity to rot in the ruins.
*   **The Methods:** Propaganda, secret police (The Censors), and the weaponization of local horrors. If a region like Brambleford is failing, the Hegemony will let it fall to use as a warning, or wait until the people are desperate enough to accept "emergency" Hegemony rule.

## The Player's Role: The Truth-Binder
Players begin as "Useful Hands" (Act 1), but they are destined to become **Truth-Binders**.
1.  **Phase 1 (Discovery):** Realizing the Hegemony's maps and history books don't match the ruins they find.
2.  **Phase 2 (Reclamation):** Learning the ancient rituals to "Soar" (temporarily appease) local Primal spirits to restore short-term order.
3.  **Phase 3 (Conflict):** Actively opposing Hegemony Censors and freeing the Caged Sovereigns.

## The Mystery Engine: Guidelines for Content
To maintain the air of mystery, all content should follow these rules:

1.  **Unreliable Narrators:** NPCs should often contradict each other based on their education (Taught History) vs. their lived experience (True History).
2.  **Environmental Dissonance:** A ruin that "officially" was a temple to a Hegemony hero should have carvings or architecture that suggest it was actually a maintenance station for the Vow.
3.  **The "Third Side":** There should always be a third party (e.g., the Goblins, the Barrows) who are reacting to the crisis in ways that reveal a piece of the truth if the player looks closely.
4.  **Censored Knowledge:** "Readables" should often have portions "Redacted by the Office of Civic Truth." Players might need to find the missing scraps in dangerous places.

## Scaling for MMORPG Content
The "Broken Vow" allows for endless horizontal expansion:
*   **Factions:** The Hegemony Loyalists, The Vow-Keepers (rebels), The Primalists (who want to destroy all civilization), and Local Neutral Parties.
*   **World Events:** "The Fraying" can intensify in specific zones, requiring players to perform massive group rituals to keep the town from being swallowed by the wild.
*   **Legendary Loot:** Items are not just "magic," they are "Vow-Relics" that have their own requirements or "Favors" they demand from the wielder.
