# Brave Act 2 Production Batch: Chapters 4 & 5 - Under the Spire

## Intent
To reveal the technical reality of the "Siphon" and introduce the player to the organized resistance (Vow-Keepers).

---

## 1. Rooms & World Topology
### Chapter 4: The Siphon’s Shadow
- **`the_under_spire_access`**: A service elevator disguised as a maintenance closet in the High-Quarter.
- **`siphon_intake_alpha`**: A massive chamber where blackwater pipes converge. The air hums with a vibration that makes your teeth ache.
- **`cooling_vats_primary`**: Subterranean pools of glowing, siphoned energy. Dangerous to touch.
- **`the_scream_manifold`**: A junction point where the "voice" of the land can be heard through the pipes.

### Chapter 5: The Vow-Keepers
- **`the_hidden_lamp_house`**: A sanctuary built into the old foundations of Oakhaven. Filled with books and ancient lanterns.
- **`sewer_junction_theta`**: The complex network of tunnels used by the resistance to move through the city.
- **`the_strategy_room`**: A map-filled room where the Vow-Keepers plan their strikes.

---

## 2. Quests
### Chapter 4 Quests
- **`vibration_of_the_void`**
    - **Giver:** Curator Elara.
    - **Objective:** Use a "Tuning Fork" at the Scream Manifold to record the frequency of the siphoning.
    - **Summary:** The land is screaming. We need proof to convince the people.
- **`sabotage_intake_alpha`**
    - **Giver:** Vow-Keeper Contact (Shadowy figure).
    - **Objective:** Overload one of the siphoning valves in Intake Alpha.
    - **Summary:** A small blow against the city's power grid.

### Chapter 5 Quests
- **`the_first_hollow_sign`**
    - **Giver:** The Elder (Vow-Keeper Leader).
    - **Objective:** Learn the first "Hollow-Sign" by completing a series of ancient rituals in the Hidden Lamp-House.
    - **Summary:** Initiation into the resistance.
- **`recruitment_drive`**
    - **Giver:** Kaelen (Resistance liaison).
    - **Objective:** Find 3 "Potential Recruits" in the Silt Slums and give them the Hollow-Sign.
    - **Summary:** Growing the movement from the shadows.

---

## 3. NPCs
- **`the_elder`**:
    - **Location:** `the_hidden_lamp_house`
    - **Vibe:** Ancient, calm, and deeply saddened. A former High Architect who defected.
- **`sentry_prime_krax`**:
    - **Location:** `siphon_intake_alpha`
    - **Vibe:** An "Augmented" Hegemony soldier. More machine than man. (Mini-boss).
- **`scout_nara`**:
    - **Location:** `sewer_junction_theta`
    - **Vibe:** Fast-talking, energetic. The resistance's primary courier.

---

## 4. Enemies & Encounters
- **`augmented_guard`**: Humans fused with iron and siphoned power. High defense.
- **`siphon_leech`**: A bloated, slug-like creature that eats the leaks in the siphoning pipes.
- **`security_pulse`**: An environmental hazard (trap) that fires beams of pure siphoned light.

---

## 5. Items
- **`vow_tuning_fork`**: (Quest Item) Used to interact with siphoning machinery.
- **`hollow_sign_token`**: (Accessory) Marks the player as a Vow-Keeper member.
- **`blackwater_residue`**: (Material) A byproduct of siphoning, used in high-level Act 2 crafting.
